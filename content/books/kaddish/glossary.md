Glossary
========

Here follows a glossary of terms in mostly alphabetical order — ‘mostly’ because some concepts have been surfaced to the top of the list in order to provide clarity.

*   **The System** — more generally, the first successful computer system hosting the uplodaed consciousnesses of humans. As it was originally constructed under a great deal of secrecy, the project was referred to in only the vaguest of terms to keep the whole obscured from developers, participants, and outside governments. Used more commonly by older uploads. More specifically, it may be used as a somewhat dated term for Lagrange.
    
*   **sim** — a contiguous location within the System. This may be as small as a room, or as large as the world (or larger) and contain multiple buildings. For instance, Beth Tikvah is a sim that contains a mountain range, a small town built around and on the slopes of a butte at the end of the range, and some generative prairie surrounding it all. Completely separate from that is the Second Temple sim visited at one point. Two sims can be connected via a doorway of sorts, but they are not contiguous.
    
*   forking
    
*   clade
    
*   cladist
    
*   instance
    
*   **Lagrange** — the instance of the System constructed at the fifth Earth–Moon Lagrange Point (L5). It is the original of the three extant instances of the System, with two smaller copies, Castor and Pollux, patterned off of it.
    
*   **Castor and Pollux** — two instances of the System, smaller than Lagrange, which were launched away from their predecessor in 2325, each traveling in opposite directions out of the Solar System. Not everyone volunteered to join the mission, and transmission delays make communication difficult, so after 75 years, they are thought of mostly as a curiosity.
    
*   sensorium message
    
*   **systime** — a way of specifying date and time sys-side that is integral to Lagrange (and thus Castor and Pollux) rather than reliant on Earth. It is written in the format _systime YEAR+DAY,_ where YEAR is the number of years since 2124 and DAY is the day of the year (with optional decimal points to express time. For instance, systime 0+0 is January 1st, 2124, and the Century Attack occurred at systime 275+365.99, or at midnight on December 31st, 2399.
    
*   the exchange
    
*   sys-side/phys-side
    
*   oneirotecture
    
*   oneiroimpressionism
    
*   **CPV** — short for ‘contraproprioceptive virus’; a virus that caused the uploaded mind to lose track of its sense of a body. Those affected would either crash or quit out of agony within the span of a few seconds. Incredibly difficult to manufacture as it needed to be tailored to an individual, but it was the only known way to kill a cladist. Patched out during the downtime that followed the Century Attack.
    
*   **Ansible** — the mechanism by which human minds are uploaded to Lagrange. Although it refers specifically to the communication protocol, it has come to refer to the whole setup: one goes to an ‘Ansible clinic’ to upload, and uploading has been called colloquially ‘going the Ansible’.
    
*   Perisystem
    
*   Fennecism
    
*   sensewright
    
*   **SysCon** — short for ‘The System Consortium’; the organizational body for everything pertaining to the System.
    

*   **yerida** — emigration from Israel; lit. ‘descent’. Cf. _aliyah_, emigration to Israel; lit ‘ascent’.
    
*   **doikayt** — the idea that Jews in the Diaspora can feel a sense of belonging wherever they are; lit. ‘hereness’.
    
*   **golah** — the Jewish Diaspora.
    
*   **_Mishkan HaNetzach_** — the primary resource within the ANRC that acts both as a siddur (prayer book) and guide to Jewish life for New Reform Jews. As a living document, it contains continually up-to-date responses to various questions asked about living Jewishly, both sys- and phys-side. The name translates to ‘The Eternal Tabernacle’.
    
*   **Kaddish** — A prayer said at several times throughout Jewish prayer services. Often, however, it refers to the Mourner’s Kaddish, the prayer said when remembering the dead. Notably, the prayer does not mention death at all, but instead celebrates and praises God.
    
*   **_Mechayeh HaMetim/HaKol_** — names for the divine. _Mechayeh HaMetim_ means ‘who gives live to the dead’, whereas _Mechayeh HaKol_ means ‘who gives life to all’.
    
*   **_Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh_** — a name for the divine taken from Exodus 3:14: _“And God said to Moses, “Ehyeh-Asher-Ehyeh,” continuing, “Thus shall you say to the Israelites, ‘Ehyeh sent me to you.’”_ A rough translation might be ‘I am that I am’ or ‘I will be what I will be’.
    
*   **_Hayan Hoveh v’Yihye_** — a name for the divine meaning ‘was, is, and will be’.
    
*   **The ANRC** — short for ‘the Association of New Reform Congregations’; the central organizational body for New Reform Judaism, including the rabbinical council, a publishing arm, and so on.
    
*   Yom HaShichzur
    
*   Shavuot
    
*   Shabbat
    
*   Pesach
    
*   Sefirat HaOmer
    
*   Yom HaShoah
    

The Mourner’s Kaddish
=====================

Here follows the text of the Mourner’s Kaddish referenced several times within the text as it appears in _Mishkan HaNetzach._

> Yitgadal v’yitkadash sh’mei raba. B’alma di v’ra chirutei, v’yamlich malchutei, b’chayeichon  
> uv’yomeichon uv’chayei d’chol beit Yisrael, baagala uviz’man kariv. V’im’ru: Amen.  
> Y’hei sh’mei raba m’varach l’alam ul’almei almaya.  
> Yitbarach v’yishtabach v’yitpaar v’yitromam v’yitnasei, v’yit’hadar v’yitaleh v’yit’halal sh’mei d’Kud’sha  
> B’rich Hu, l’eila min kol birchata v’shirata, tushb’chata v’nechemata, daamiran b’alma. V’imru: Amen.  
> Y’hei sh’lama raba min sh’maya, v’chayim aleinu v’al kol Yisrael. V’imru: Amen.  
> Oseh shalom bimromav, Hu yaaseh shalom aleinu, v’al kol Yisrael, v’al kol yoshvei yekum. V’imru: Amen.
> 
> Exalted and hallowed be God’s great name in the world which God created, according to plan.  
> May God’s majesty be revealed in the days of our lifetime and the life of all Israel—speedily, imminently. To which we say: Amen.  
> Blessed be God’s great name to all eternity.  
> Blessed, praised, honored, exalted, extolled, glorified, adored, and lauded be the name of the  
> Holy Blessed One, beyond all earthly words and songs of blessing, praise, and comfort. To which we say: Amen.  
> May there be abundant peace from heaven, and life, for us and all Israel. To which we say: Amen.  
> May the One who creates harmony on high, bring peace us, to all Israel, and to all of creation. To which we say: Amen

A basic timeline of events
==========================

*   **The Century Attack**
    
*   **The murder of True Name**
    
*   **Convergence**
    
*   **Launch**
    
*   **Secession**
    
*   **The Twelve Hours’ War** — a short battle between members of the New Zion government of Israel and the neighboring Lebanon and Syriah. See _The Israeli Crisis_
    
*   **The Israeli Crisis** — During the late 2000s, as America’s power waned and the world saw consolidation of nations under unions such as the Western Federation and Sino-Russian Bloc, Israel remained largely independent. However, into the 2100s, it aligned itself with the S-R Bloc. As it began to act out in aggression, the S-R Bloc was treaty-bound to assist (or at least not get in the way). This culminated in the Twelve Hours’ War and the subseqent peace talks, mediated by Lagrange.
